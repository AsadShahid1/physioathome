5 Essential Exercises to Relieve Lower Back & Sciatica Pain at Home

By Dr. Hina Asif PT • 5 Min Read

Lower back pain and sciatica nerve discomfort are among the most common issues reported by our patients in Lahore. When acute pain strikes, traveling to a clinic can aggravate symptoms.

1. Knee-to-Chest Stretch

Lie on your back and pull one knee up gently towards your chest. Hold for 20-30 seconds.

2. McKenzie Spine Extension

Lie face down and gently press up through your palms while keeping hips flat.
